## Optical edition

The viewing angle now sets the width and weight of the opening title, the color of the controls, and the position of a diagonal exposure on the plate. The nine-panel fold uses the outgoing word’s alpha mask to reveal an overprint in the gaps. In Studio, the field being edited determines the live impression behind the form. Hovering or focusing each Lab category previews titles in that directory. [Visual references and design decisions](https://ejaubashi.github.io/t018/exhibit/design/#visual-references)

## Motion between sections

The project reads as three sections. Between Exhibit and Studio, nine hinged planes each carry a slice of two full-width words. At their resting angles, the slices register as complete readings. During the turn the viewer sees both sides and the gaps between the panels. Submitting the Studio form carries the edited word back into the sculpture and changes both printed faces. The Lab word moves into the directory heading across documents. [Seam Transitions](https://github.com/codrops/SeamTransitions) · [View Transitions](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/View_Transition_API)

The fold uses a root scroll timeline over the exact distance that the panel stays pinned. Dragging the range control moves the page through those same positions. Each panel’s registered index offsets its rotation and aligns its fragment with the rest of the word. Stored indices and a scroll listener supply the same geometry where the CSS features are unavailable. Reduced motion shows the completed print and removes the pinning distance. [Scroll timelines](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/Properties/animation-timeline/scroll) · [Sibling index](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/Values/sibling-index) · [3D transforms](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/Properties/transform-style)

## Motion studies

Researched on 26 November 2026 for **Two readings**. The November Codrops character studies use separate treatments for groups of letters, including WebGL distortions. Gooey Sticky Menu uses the position of an edge to control when a deformation stretches and breaks. In Typefield, the corresponding inputs are angular speed, distance from the pointer, and time since a strike. The words give the motion a recognizable starting point and a readable ending. [Text Character Animations](https://github.com/codrops/TextCharacterAnimations/tree/ac0fe97a4e4f786ef5d514d7030b6eb5855da05b) · [Gooey Sticky Menu](https://github.com/codrops/GooeyStickyMenu)

| Study | Finding and implementation |
| --- | --- |
| Momentum | Coalesced Pointer Events recover positions sampled between dispatches. Typefield estimates release speed over the last 88 ms and gives a critically damped spring that velocity. The scene can settle at either readable angle or anywhere between them. Browsers without coalesced samples use the dispatched events. [Pointer Events Level 3](https://www.w3.org/TR/pointerevents3/#dom-pointerevent-getcoalescedevents) · [Motion spring reference](https://github.com/motiondivision/motion/blob/main/packages/motion-dom/src/animation/generators/spring.ts) |
| Lens | Imbrizi’s interactive particles use a local influence field to make the pointer affect nearby marks. In Typefield, distance sets the reach, and the character’s 3D depth sets how far it slips sideways. Separating warm and cool copies around a letter makes that refraction visible. [Interactive Particles](https://github.com/brunoimbrizi/interactive-particles) |
| Resonance | Each strike creates an expanding wave with a Gaussian envelope and a cosine crest. A second strike adds its displacement to the first at every letter. Where the crests meet, their push can strengthen or cancel. The waveform runs on elapsed time so it carries the same distance at a given time across refresh rates. |
| Exposure | A trace is easier to read when its offsets track the actual turn. Typefield keeps a short history and draws three positions from 68, 152, and 245 ms earlier. The traces change direction with the gesture and clear once those earlier angles catch up. |
| Motion score | Web Animations exposes `currentTime` for seeking while paused. The score follows that model with an elapsed-time function that computes the camera, lens, waves, and exposure from the same playhead. Scrubbing backward to a time produces the same image as arriving there during playback. [Animation.currentTime](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Animation/currentTime) |

The stable sculpture uses CSS 3D and DOM text. During a turn or warp, the motion renderer projects the same 3D coordinates and reuses cached glyphs from an `OffscreenCanvas` atlas, with a detached canvas fallback. This keeps the moving text in one drawing surface and avoids recomputing over a thousand DOM transforms per frame. The renderer rests when the scene settles. The desktop compatibility data records `getCoalescedEvents()` in Chrome 58, Firefox 59, and Safari 18.2, and `OffscreenCanvas` in Chrome 69, Firefox 105, and Safari 16.4. [OffscreenCanvas](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/OffscreenCanvas) · [PointerEvent data](https://github.com/mdn/browser-compat-data/blob/main/api/PointerEvent.json) · [OffscreenCanvas data](https://github.com/mdn/browser-compat-data/blob/main/api/OffscreenCanvas.json)

The score starts on request. Each study has a keyboard-operable demonstration. Reduced motion keeps direct angle control, still versions of the optical effects, and manual seeking through the score. [WCAG 2.2 Animation from Interactions](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions.html)

## Design direction

Build a kinetic type specimen. Give the hero a single oversized sentence, then let readers change its weight, width, and composition. Use a quiet editorial section to reward scrolling. Typefield pairs Archivo’s broad letterforms with small IBM Plex Mono control labels. Near-black, warm paper, and an acid-green accent make the reading section feel distinct from the playground. [Archivo](https://github.com/Omnibus-Type/Archivo) · [IBM Plex](https://github.com/IBM/plex)

Assign each gesture a visible consequence. A pointer gives nearby letters more weight, a button replays the phrase as a brief decode, and an underlined word opens a note at the point of reading. Keep long passages still enough to read and reserve the largest movement for the display type.

## Interactions worth building now

| Interaction | How it fits a text-only site | Browser technique |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Variable-font field** | Let the pointer press into letters and release them. Give readers sliders for deliberate, keyboard-accessible changes. | Use a typeface with `wght` and `wdth` axes. Register a numeric `@property` for the live weight offset so it eases back independently of the sliders. For a different character, try a face with `opsz` for size-specific shapes or `GRAD` for changes in stroke intensity with steadier spacing. [Font variations](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/Properties/font-variation-settings) · [Registered properties](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/At-rules/@property) · [Roboto Flex axes](https://github.com/googlefonts/roboto-flex) |
| **Composition morph** | Make the same headline feel like two posters by changing alignment, width, or color. The transition lets the reader follow the phrase through the change. | `document.startViewTransition()` captures two states. Shared `view-transition-name` values connect the lines. Available from Chrome 111, Firefox 144, and Safari 18. An instant layout change is the fallback. [View Transition API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/View_Transition_API) |
| **Scroll-driven ink** | Let lines settle into full contrast as they enter the viewport. Good for bringing a chapter into view at the reader’s pace. | `animation-timeline: view()` with `animation-range` follows the element through the viewport. Available from Chrome 115, Firefox 159, and Safari 26. Feature-detect it and let the copy render at full contrast on older browsers. [Scroll-driven animations](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Guides/Scroll-driven_animations) |
| **Annotations beside a word** | Make a sentence operate as navigation. A short definition or aside opens where the reader encounters the term. | Native `popover` handles opening, Escape, and outside-click dismissal. `position-area` and `position-try-fallbacks` tether it to a word and flip it when there’s limited room. `position-area` is available from Chrome 129, Firefox 147, and Safari 26. [Popover API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Popover_API) · [CSS anchor positioning](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Guides/Anchor_positioning) |
| **Character resolve** | Give a title or section change a brief proofreader or terminal effect. Keep the surrounding words stable so the eye has an anchor. | Segment display text with `Intl.Segmenter`, reserve each glyph’s layout space, and keep a stable accessible heading. For a larger project with animated line breaks, GSAP SplitText offers responsive re-splitting and an `onSplit` callback. [Segmenter](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/JavaScript/Reference/Global_Objects/Intl/Segmenter) · [SplitText](https://gsap.com/docs/v3/Plugins/SplitText/) |

## Newer features to explore

- **Element-scoped view transitions.** Animate a single specimen while the buttons and the rest of the page remain available. `Element.startViewTransition()` is experimental and available from Chrome 147 in the checked dataset. Typefield uses it where present, then falls back to the document API or a direct update. [Element-scoped transitions](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Element/startViewTransition)
- **Stagger from DOM order.** `calc((sibling-index() - 1) * 35ms)` can delay each item in a run of letters or lines. The function is recorded from Chrome 138, Firefox 154, and Safari 26.2. Pair it with `text-box-trim` and `text-box-edge` when a type grid also needs tighter alignment against cap-height or baseline. [Sibling index](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/Values/sibling-index) · [Text box trim](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/Properties/text-box-trim)
- **Play on entry.** A scroll timeline scrubs through an effect as the reader moves. `animation-trigger` with `timeline-trigger` can fire a time-based sequence when a section crosses a threshold, including a single play-through. The checked data records it from Chrome 146 with Firefox and Safari support still absent. [Animation triggers](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/Properties/animation-trigger)
- **Give notes a short entrance and exit.** `@starting-style` supplies the first visible frame. `transition-behavior: allow-discrete` lets `display` and the top-layer `overlay` change at the right moment. Typefield uses this for the anchored notes. [Starting style](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/At-rules/@starting-style) · [Transition behavior](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/Properties/transition-behavior)

## Recent design references

- **Codrops Text Character Animations, November 2026 source.** Cursor Scramble, Proofread, and Overtype draw their motion from familiar acts of writing and editing. For Typefield, that suggests making a scramble resolve toward a readable phrase. [Source and demo](https://github.com/codrops/TextCharacterAnimations/tree/ac0fe97a4e4f786ef5d514d7030b6eb5855da05b)
- **Codrops Inline Hover Reveals, November 2026 source.** Words inside running copy become entry points for different reveals. A text-only version can keep the reveal itself typographic, such as a definition, footnote, or second reading. Make it a button so the same gesture works by keypress and tap. [Source and demo](https://github.com/codrops/InlineHoverReveals/tree/26e8bc257c9c64e77d3204479ec37e49ae2d3483)
- **Codrops On-Scroll Text Motion, December 2025 source.** Type blocks are the moving composition. The practical takeaway is to tune one effect to the scale of each passage, from a quiet line entrance to a prominent headline reflow. [Source and demo](https://github.com/codrops/ScrollTextMotion/tree/9f05d938f7b38e76e3146f26e393118e7975b6b3)
