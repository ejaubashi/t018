Text
behaves.

Make a font. Bend letters with sound. Follow a footnote until it becomes a room of its own.

  1. Cast Draw the contours of a font and take the typeface with you. ↗
  2. Frequency Turn sound into changing weight, width, and height. ↗
  3. Descent Follow a word into a chapter you can share. ↗
  4. Counterpoint Pair two fragments of testimony and question what they say together. ↗

More experiments

  1. Gravity Hold the text to pull its letters. Release and watch them find their places. ↗
  2. Fold Scroll the sentence to turn four lines into a different reading. ↗
  3. Underprint Sweep a lens across a sentence to find the one underneath. ↗
  4. Echo Type a line, erase something, and replay the traces of your edits. ↗
  5. Exchange Give a different word the lead and watch the sentence reorganize. ↗
