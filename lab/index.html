Text
behaves.

Pull a letter out of place, turn a sentence inside out, or replay the moment you changed your mind.

  1. Gravity Hold the text to pull its letters. Release and watch them find their places. ↗
  2. Fold Scroll the sentence to turn four lines into a different reading. ↗
  3. Underprint Sweep a lens across a sentence to find the one underneath. ↗
  4. Echo Type a line, erase something, and replay the traces of your edits. ↗
  5. Exchange Give a different word the lead and watch the sentence reorganize. ↗
