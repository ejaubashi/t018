Implementation

A CSS scroll timeline drives four 3D text panels through staggered ranges. The slider and button move the same scroller as a finger or wheel. A scroll listener supplies the rotation on browsers without timelines. Reduced motion switches between the two readings at the midpoint. Scroll timelines
